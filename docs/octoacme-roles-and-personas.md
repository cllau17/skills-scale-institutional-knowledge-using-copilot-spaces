# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
The Change Manager oversees and coordinates process or scope changes within the project, ensuring minimal disruption and proper communication of proposed changes across all teams. They assess the impact of change requests and guide the organization through transitions.

### Responsibilities
- Evaluate and document change requests for scope, process, or technology
- Coordinate impact assessments with Project Manager and team leads
- Communicate proposed changes to affected stakeholders and obtain approvals
- Maintain a change log and track implementation status
- Ensure rollback or contingency plans are in place for significant changes

### Goals
- Minimize disruption caused by changes
- Ensure all changes are communicated, approved, and tracked
- Maintain project stability while accommodating necessary adjustments

### Typical Communication
- Change request reviews with Project Manager and Product Manager
- Impact assessment meetings with Team Leads
- Change notifications and approvals to stakeholder groups
- Updates to the risk register for change-related risks

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **Project Manager**: coordinates scheduling and resource impact of changes
- **Product Manager**: aligns scope changes with product goals
- **Communications Lead**: ensures change communications reach all stakeholders
- **Team Leads**: assesses technical or operational impact

---

## Communications Lead

### Role Summary
The Communications Lead develops, documents, and implements communication strategies for the project. They ensure timely and consistent updates reach relevant stakeholders and manage the overall messaging cadence across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the project communication plan
- Draft and distribute status updates, announcements, and release notes
- Coordinate messaging with the Stakeholder Liaison and Project Manager
- Manage communication channels (email, Slack, internal wiki, etc.)
- Tailor communications for different audiences (technical, executive, end-user)

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholders receive accurate, timely, and relevant information
- Maintain a single source of truth for project updates
- Reduce information gaps and miscommunication across teams

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and newsletters
- Release announcements and go-live notifications
- Meeting notes and decision summaries distributed after key meetings
- Escalation communications when risks or issues arise

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **Project Manager**: aligns on status and key messages for stakeholder updates
- **Stakeholder Liaison**: gathers stakeholder feedback to inform communications
- **Change Manager**: coordinates messaging for change announcements
- **Product Manager**: ensures product-level messaging is accurate

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
The Stakeholder Liaison represents stakeholder interests throughout the project lifecycle. They gather requirements, feedback, and concerns from stakeholders, and ensure alignment between external stakeholders and the project team.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and map all key stakeholder groups and their communication needs
- Gather and document stakeholder requirements, expectations, and feedback
- Serve as the primary point of contact between stakeholders and the project team
- Facilitate stakeholder review sessions and approvals
- Escalate unresolved stakeholder concerns to the Project Manager

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder voices are heard and factored into project decisions
- Reduce misalignment between stakeholder expectations and project deliverables
- Build and maintain trust between stakeholders and the project team

### Typical Communication
- Regular stakeholder briefings and update meetings
- Feedback collection via surveys, interviews, or review sessions
- Escalation reports to the Project Manager when concerns are unresolved

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **Communications Lead**: provides stakeholder feedback to shape communications
- **Product Manager**: shares stakeholder requirements and priorities
- **Project Manager**: escalates blocking stakeholder issues and dependencies
- **Change Manager**: surfaces stakeholder concerns related to proposed changes

---

## QA Analyst

### Role Summary
The QA Analyst ensures the quality of project deliverables by validating requirements, designing and executing test plans, and collaborating with development and release teams to identify and resolve defects before deployment.

### Responsibilities
- Review and validate acceptance criteria and requirements for testability
- Create test plans, test cases, and test scripts
- Execute manual and automated tests, and document results
- Track, report, and verify resolution of defects
- Collaborate with Developers on root cause analysis and regression testing
- Participate in release readiness reviews

### Goals
- Ensure deliverables meet defined acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce defect leakage to production
- Improve overall product reliability through systematic testing

### Typical Communication
- Test plan and test case reviews with Developers and Product Manager
- Defect triage meetings with the development team
- Release readiness sign-off reports
- Quality metrics summaries for Project Manager and stakeholders

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **Developers**: coordinates on defect resolution and regression testing
- **Product Manager**: validates acceptance criteria and expected behavior
- **Project Manager**: reports quality status and any release-blocking issues
- **Release/Deployment team**: confirms readiness for deployments

---

## Agile Coach

### Role Summary
The Agile Coach advises teams on best Agile practices, facilitates retrospectives and other Agile ceremonies, and supports continuous improvement by helping project teams adopt and refine effective workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Guide teams in adopting and refining Agile frameworks (Scrum, Kanban, SAFe, etc.)
- Facilitate sprint planning, retrospectives, and other Agile ceremonies
- Identify and address process inefficiencies and anti-patterns
- Coach team members and leadership on Agile mindset and principles
- Support the Project Manager in removing organizational impediments

### Goals
- Build a culture of continuous improvement within teams
- Improve team velocity, predictability, and delivery quality
- Reduce friction in cross-team collaboration and workflow

### Typical Communication
- Sprint retrospectives and process review sessions
- One-on-one coaching sessions with team members and leads
- Agile maturity assessments and improvement roadmaps
- Workshop facilitation for new teams or new processes

### Collaboration Touchpoints
- **Project Manager**: advises on process improvements and escalates impediments
- **Team Leads and Developers**: coaches on Agile ceremonies and practices
- **Product Manager**: aligns on backlog refinement and prioritization practices
- **Stakeholder Liaison**: ensures stakeholder engagement practices align with Agile principles
